A nudge, without sound.

It asks about your task, never about where you went.

  1. It reads the window in front.

    The app, title and page address, only during a session. Messages and music are never read.

  2. It compares with your task.

    Your task and project notes, judged by Apple’s on-device model. No cloud model.

  3. Two checks have to agree.

    One reading is never enough. A second check has to land the same way.

  4. The drift has to last.

    A quick look away is fine. Hushd waits to see if you come back on your own.

  5. Then, a gentle nudge.

    In the notch, without sound, and never naming the distraction.

The ring speaks in shapes.

Shape first, color second, so a state never rests on color alone.
