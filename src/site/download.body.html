Tell us what it got wrong.

In Hushd, click your name, then Send feedback. It saves a report you read first, and opens a GitHub form for you to drag it into. Hushd never sends a report by itself.

A report holds

  • Your words
  • The build, your Mac’s model and macOS version
  • Whether permissions are on, and your settings switches
  • Counts, and when things happened
  • Hushd’s last calls: on, unsure or off

It never holds

  • Task or project names
  • Window titles
  • Web addresses
  • Words from your screen
  • Pictures

The app, window title and page address of the window in front, only during a session. If you turn on screen reading, it also reads the words in that window. The model sees words, never the picture.

Hushd judges with Apple’s on-device model. There is no account, no cloud service of ours and no analytics. Pictures and screen words stay in memory and are dropped when the window changes. Two small checks go to hushd.store, our own site: your beta key (with a code for this Mac and the app version) when it opens and about once a day, and a look for a newer build. Neither carries anything about your work. Two optional features use iCloud, and both stay off until you turn them on: Friends shares your name, avatar and daily totals with friends you invite, and iPhone sync keeps your numbers and task names in your own iCloud.
